Open Camera handoff issue

Observed launch from Open Camera to `org.openscreen.controlcast` used:
- action: `com.android.camera.action.REVIEW`
- data: `content://media/external/video/media/2366`
- flags: `0x3800000`

Problem:
- The intent did not include `FLAG_GRANT_READ_URI_PERMISSION` (`0x1`).
- Without that temporary URI grant, the receiving app cannot rely on direct
  access to the `content://` video URI unless it separately holds media read
  permission.

Effect:
- The app can appear in the chooser and launch correctly.
- The selected video may still fail to open because the URI itself is not
  readable to the receiving app.

Expected fix on sender side:
- When Open Camera starts the review/view intent for a captured video URI, it
  should add `Intent.FLAG_GRANT_READ_URI_PERMISSION`.

Receiver-side mitigation implemented here:
- Request `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO` on Android 13+ or `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` on older
  Android versions when a shared `content://` video arrives without sufficient
  access, then retry opening the URI.
