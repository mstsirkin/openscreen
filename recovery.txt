Seek recovery plan

1. Measure the transition cost.
Record one attributable run for single off-keyframe seek and one for seek storm, capturing before/after screenshots plus DEBUG_PRINT and mode-switch logs. The goal is to quantify:
- how long fallback stays active
- whether the disruption is a blink, pause, or visible jump
- whether cast/exo drift grows during the transition

2. Reduce visible disruption during temporary fallback.
Keep recovery in-session, but smooth the handoff:
- avoid unnecessary pause/armed states during mode switches
- keep local Exo pinned to cast-authoritative playback state during fallback/re-entry
- minimize UI-visible resets when switching passthrough -> fallback -> passthrough

3. Tackle backlog pressure during aggressive scrubbing.
Refine seek-storm handling so stale media does not dominate output:
- coalesce rapid seeks more aggressively
- keep only a small amount of useful in-flight continuity during drag
- once pressure crosses threshold, restart media production from the newest seek target without tearing down the cast session

4. Add better debug proof for the scrubbing path.
Keep DEBUG_PRINT, and extend/use it to confirm:
- current mode
- whether sender was reclaimed
- in-flight duration vs max
- latest seek target vs cast position
This is to prove whether each change reduces backlog instead of just changing symptoms.

5. Validate on-device with the same repros.
For each committed build:
- test single seek
- test seek storm
- verify:
  - no crash
  - no disconnect
  - cast resumes visibly
  - passthrough really returns
  - disruption is reduced
  - backlog pressure is improved, not just hidden
